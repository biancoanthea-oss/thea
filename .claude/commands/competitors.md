Analyse competitor: $ARGUMENTS
(If none is named, work through `marketing/seo/competitor-analysis.md → The
competitor set` and ask which one, or take the first without a dated snapshot.)

Follow the six sections in `marketing/seo/competitor-analysis.md`: positioning,
pricing, features, SEO footprint, ads, social proof.

**Hard rule: work from live pages, not memory.** Fetch their homepage, pricing
page, and a representative content page. Quote what is actually on them. Every
pricing or feature claim carries today's date. Anything you could not verify is
flagged as unverified — do not fill the gap with a plausible guess.

Build the feature matrix against `marketing/brief.md §1`, and answer honestly:
what do they have that a buyer would genuinely care about and we don't?

Prioritise their **negative reviews** if you can reach any. The complaints in a
competitor's 2-star reviews are the most directly usable input in the whole
analysis — each one maps to a landing page headline.

End with three specific, ranked changes we should make, each with the reason and
the expected effect. Save to
`marketing/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-competitor-<name>.md`.
