## Boilerplate paragraphs

Adapt, don't paste blindly — an evaluator who has read the same paragraph in
three bids notices.

### One-line description (≈25 words)

[NAME] is a [LEGAL FORM] established in [YEAR], delivering [SERVICE] to
[CUSTOMER TYPE] across [GEOGRAPHY].

### Short profile (≈75 words)

[NAME] has delivered [SERVICE] since [YEAR]. We employ [N] people, of whom [N]
are [ROLE], and work with [CUSTOMER TYPES] including [EXAMPLES]. In the last
[N] years we have completed [N] contracts of comparable scope, including
[REFERENCE], where we [SPECIFIC OUTCOME WITH A NUMBER]. We hold [KEY
CERTIFICATIONS] and operate a [QUALITY SYSTEM].

### Full profile (≈200 words)

[Expand the above: history and stability, delivery model, key people and their
experience, quality and governance arrangements, relevant sector experience
with named contracts and measurable outcomes, and what distinguishes the way
we work. Close with a sentence tying it back to this buyer's requirement —
that last sentence gets rewritten every time.]
