Tenders repeatedly ask for the same documents. Track them here so you know
what exists, where it lives, and whether it's still in date — chasing an
expired certificate the day before a deadline is a self-inflicted wound.

## Notes

- Policies should be signed, dated and within their review period. An undated
  policy or one reviewed four years ago reads as a document written for
  tenders rather than one that's used.
- Keep a PDF of each, named plainly (`Health-and-Safety-Policy-2026.pdf`).
  Buyers reject files they can't identify.
- Where a buyer specifies a policy you don't have, don't fake it — either
  write and adopt it properly before submission, or state the position
  honestly and describe the equivalent arrangements in place.
