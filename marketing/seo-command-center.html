Watch for brand fragmentation. You have more than one property covering the same service (AV on both stackedav.ie and audiovisual.ie; merch on stackedmerchandise.ie, merch.stacked.ie and shop.stacked.ie). Splitting one topic across domains splits your ranking authority and risks duplicate content. Decide on one canonical home per service and 301-redirect the rest, or set canonical tags.
