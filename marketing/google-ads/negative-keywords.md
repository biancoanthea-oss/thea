# Negative keywords

## The logic (read this before adding anything)

Wedding vocabulary is enormous and mostly not ours. Someone searching "wedding
photos" could be looking for a photographer, a venue portfolio, free stock
images, editing software, printing, or a job. Our product occupies a narrow
slice: **the couple, buying a tool for their guests to upload with.**

Four families of waste, and the rule for each:

| Family | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Wrong buyer** | Professionals, vendors, venues, and job seekers. They search the same nouns for different reasons |
| **Wrong product** | Photographers, printing, albums (physical), editing, dresses, invitations, cameras (hardware) |
| **Free intent** | "free", "download free", "template", "stock" — will never pay |
| **Research intent** | "meaning", "history", "reddit", "ideas" (mostly), "diy", "how to make" — informational, belongs to SEO not to paid |

**The one thing not to do:** do not negative anything in
`brief.md §5 Protected terms`. Those look expensive before they look profitable.

## Ad-group cross-negatives

To keep traffic in the right ad group with the right ad:

- In **AG5 Problem-aware**, add the AG1/AG2 core terms as exact negatives, so
  product-intent searches land on the sales page rather than a blog post.
- In **AG1 Core**, add `qr code` as a phrase negative if AG2 is under-delivering
  — this forces QR-intent traffic into its own better-matched ad.
- In **AG4 Competitor**, no cross-negatives; exact match already contains it.

---

## Weekly maintenance

Run `/search-terms`. For each wasteful query, ask which of the four families it
belongs to and add the **root word**, not the full query — one good root negative
kills a hundred future variants. Adding "free wedding photo sharing app for
guests" as an exact negative blocks exactly one query; adding `free` blocks the
whole family.

**Exception:** never root-negative a word that appears in a protected term.
`wedding`, `photo`, `guest`, `share`, `app`, and `qr` are permanently off limits
as negatives.

## Pre-launch check

Add every negative **before** the first click, not after. A launch-day search
term report on an unfiltered wedding-adjacent campaign is mostly waste, and you
pay for all of it.
