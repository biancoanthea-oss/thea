# Responsive Search Ad copy

Google limits: **headlines ≤ 30 characters**, **descriptions ≤ 90**, **display
path ≤ 15 each**. Run `npm run ads:lint` from the repo root to verify every
asset in this file — it checks the fenced blocks below and fails on overruns.

Give each ad group **12–15 headlines** and **4 descriptions**. Pin nothing at
first; let Google test combinations. Only pin once you have a reason (e.g. a
legally required qualifier).

Claims here are restricted to what `../brand-voice.md → Claims policy` allows.
Anything involving price is marked `TODO` until `brief.md §2` is filled in.

## Copy principles used here

1. **Benefit before feature.** "Every guest photo, one place" beats "Cloud
   gallery with multi-upload".
2. **The objection is the guest, not the couple.** Couples worry their guests
   won't bother. Half the headlines answer that: no app, no login, one scan.
3. **Specific numbers beat adjectives.** "Ten minutes" outperforms "quick".
4. **No superlatives.** Nothing here says best, #1, or leading — all
   unsubstantiable (`brand-voice.md`).
5. **Headlines are interchangeable.** Google assembles up to 3 at a time, so no
   headline may depend on another to make sense.
6. **Descriptions each carry a full thought**, since only one or two show.
